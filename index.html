Untainted Arts

Where a work hangs changes what it says.

Untainted Arts is a house for African art. We think about how work is made, how it travels, and what it does to a room once it arrives.

Art is not decor.

A work carries a place, a hand and a reason. Hang it as an afterthought and most of that goes quiet. We start with what it is saying, then ask what the room can do for it.

Origin is not a footnote.

Who made it, where, and how it left the studio matters as much as how it looks. We go to the artist, confirm the work is theirs, and keep that record with the piece.

Borrowing is not belonging.

Being moved by a tradition is not the same as wearing it as costume. Collecting well means crediting makers fully, and knowing what a pattern or a palette meant before it became a trend.

Taste travels. Context should too.

Work moves from studio to gallery to home, and every step can strip a little away. We carry the story along with it, so the piece still makes sense on your wall.
